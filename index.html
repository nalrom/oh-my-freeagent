The Problem

AI Shouldn't Be a Luxury

Every day, talented developers are locked out of AI-powered tools because the gatekeepers demand credit cards, subscriptions, and API billing tiers. You shouldn't need a platinum credit card to use artificial intelligence.

oh-my-freeagent is built on a simple belief: AI should be free for everyone. No billing forms, no usage tiers, no "enterprise" upsells. Just working AI agents that run on genuinely free models.

We're not a trial. We're not a freemium. We're free, period.
